# Bloomberg Raw Data Quality Report

## Scope

Inspected 12 canonical series. Raw workbooks remain unchanged and local.

## Key Findings

- Bloomberg exports provide Security, date range, frequency, and PX_LAST/PX_BID fields, but no explicit unit field or Security Description.
- Rate units are inferred as percentage points from ticker meaning and observed magnitude; every converted rate retains `UNIT_INFERRED_PERCENT`.
- SOFR Index is preserved as an index and is never converted to a rate.
- USOSFR observations before 2018-04-02 are retained and flagged `PRE_SOFR_UNVERIFIED`; they are excluded from the main wide OIS sample.
- Missing values and robust jump candidates are reported, not filled, deleted, interpolated, or winsorized.
- Calendar gaps cannot be classified as holidays versus source failures without an approved market calendar; they remain unresolved.
